Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started

Q: How do I create my first packing list?

A: Simply open the app, tap the "+" button, and select "New Packing List." You can choose from our pre-made templates or start from scratch.

Q: Can I use PackItSmart without an account?

A: Yes, you can use the app locally without creating an account. However, creating an account enables cloud sync and allows you to access your lists across multiple devices.

Features & Functionality

Q: How does cloud sync work?

A: Once you create an account and enable cloud sync, all your packing lists will automatically synchronize across all your devices. Any changes you make on one device will be reflected on all others.

Q: Can I share my packing lists with others?

A: Yes! You can share your packing lists with family and friends, making it easy to coordinate trips and ensure everyone is prepared.

Q: Does the app work offline?

A: Absolutely! PackItSmart is fully functional offline. You can create, edit, and check off items without an internet connection. Your changes will sync when you're back online.

Q: How do I organize my items by category?

A: When adding items to your packing list, you can assign them to categories like Clothing, Toiletries, Electronics, Documents, etc. The app automatically groups items by category for easy organization.

Account & Security

Q: Is my data secure?

A: Yes, we take security seriously. All data is encrypted both in transit and at rest. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information. For more details, please review our Privacy Policy.

Q: Can I sign in with Google?

A: Yes, we support Google Sign-In for quick and secure authentication. You can also create an account using your email address.

Q: How do I delete my account?

A: You can delete your account at any time through the app settings. Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. Please note that this action is permanent and will delete all your data.

Troubleshooting

Q: My lists aren't syncing. What should I do?

A: First, make sure you're connected to the internet and logged into your account. Try refreshing the app by pulling down on the main screen. If the issue persists, please contact our support team.

Q: The app is crashing. How can I fix this?

A: Try closing and reopening the app. If the problem continues, check for updates in the App Store. Make sure you're running the latest version. If issues persist, contact support with details about your device and the problem.

Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

A: On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password?" and follow the instructions. You'll receive an email with a link to reset your password.

Subscriptions & Pricing

Q: Is PackItSmart free?

A: PackItSmart offers both free and premium features. The free version includes core packing list functionality. Premium subscriptions unlock advanced features like unlimited cloud storage, sharing capabilities, and an ad-free experience.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?

A: Subscriptions are managed through the App Store. To cancel, go to your device's Settings > Your Name > Subscriptions > PackItSmart, and select "Cancel Subscription."

Technical Requirements

Q: What devices are supported?

A: PackItSmart is available for iOS devices running iOS 14.0 or later. We support iPhone and iPad.

Q: Can I use PackItSmart on Android?

A: Currently, PackItSmart is available exclusively on iOS. We're exploring support for other platforms in the future.
