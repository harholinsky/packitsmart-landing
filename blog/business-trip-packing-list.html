Business Trip Packing List: What to Pack for Work Travel

Business travel requires careful planning. Forgetting work essentials can cause unnecessary stress and impact your meetings. This business trip packing list helps professionals stay prepared.

Work Essentials

  • Laptop and power cord
  • Phone and charger
  • Portable power bank
  • Headphones or earbuds
  • Connection cables (HDMI, USB-C)
  • International power adapter (if traveling abroad)
  • Business cards
  • Pen and notebook
  • USB drive or presentation materials

Documents and Identification

  • ID or passport (valid for at least 6 months if international)
  • Printed itinerary (flights, hotel, meetings)
  • Boarding passes (printed or digital)
  • Personal and business credit cards
  • Cash (including local currency if international)
  • Health insurance card
  • Travel insurance documents
  • Emergency contact list

Business Clothing

  • 2 suits, blazers, or jackets
  • 3–4 wrinkle-resistant dress shirts or tops
  • 2 pairs of dress pants, trousers, or skirts
  • 1 pair of dark jeans or casual pants (for evenings)
  • 1 light sweater or cardigan
  • 5 pairs of dress socks or tights
  • 5 sets of underwear
  • 1 pair of dress shoes
  • 1 pair of comfortable walking or athletic shoes
  • Sleepwear
  • Workout clothes and sneakers (if needed)
  • Weather-appropriate coat or jacket
  • Belt, tie, and accessories as needed

Toiletries and Grooming

  • Toothbrush, toothpaste, and dental floss
  • Shampoo, conditioner, and body wash (travel-size)
  • Deodorant
  • Razor and shaving cream
  • Face cleanser and moisturizer
  • Sunscreen and lip balm
  • Hair styling products and comb/brush
  • Contact lenses and solution (if applicable)
  • Makeup essentials (if applicable)

Health Essentials

  • Prescription medications (in original containers)
  • Over-the-counter pain relief and cold medicine
  • Hand sanitizer
  • Disinfecting wipes
  • Basic first-aid supplies (bandages, antiseptic)

Travel Comfort

  • Eye mask and earplugs
  • Travel pillow
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Packing cubes
  • Garment bag or folder for suits
  • Small umbrella
