Best Packing List for International Travel (2026 Checklist)

Packing for an international trip is stressful enough without second-guessing what you've forgotten. Here's everything you actually need, organized by category.

Use this checklist as a base and adjust it to your destination, season, and trip duration.

Travel Documents and Money

  • Passport (valid for at least 6 months beyond return date)
  • Visa (if required by destination)
  • Travel insurance documents
  • Flight tickets and hotel confirmations (printed copies)
  • Photocopies of passport and documents (kept separate from originals)
  • 2+ credit cards with no foreign transaction fees
  • Debit / ATM card
  • Cash in local currency
  • Money belt or hidden travel wallet
  • Emergency contact list (printed)
  • Vaccination card or health pass (if required)

Electronics and Chargers

  • Smartphone and charger
  • Universal international power adapter
  • Portable power bank
  • Laptop or tablet and charger (if needed)
  • Noise-canceling headphones
  • Camera with batteries, charger, and memory cards
  • AirTags or luggage trackers
  • eSIM or international SIM card
  • E-reader (optional)

Clothing

  • 5–7 underwear and socks
  • 3–4 casual t-shirts or tops
  • 2–3 nicer tops or blouses
  • 2–3 pants (jeans, trousers, or shorts depending on climate)
  • 1–2 dressy outfits for dining
  • Lightweight layers (cardigan, light sweater)
  • Rain jacket
  • Weather-appropriate coat or jacket
  • Pajamas
  • Swimsuit (if applicable)
  • 1–2 pairs of comfortable walking shoes
  • 1 pair of dressy shoes
  • Sandals or flip-flops
  • Hat and sunglasses
  • Scarf (doubles as blanket on flights)
  • Belt

Toiletries and Health

  • Toothbrush, toothpaste, and dental floss
  • Shampoo, conditioner, and body wash (travel-size, TSA-approved)
  • Deodorant
  • Face wash, moisturizer, and sunscreen (SPF 30+)
  • Lip balm with SPF
  • Razor and shaving supplies
  • Hairbrush, hair ties, and styling products
  • Contact lenses and solution (if applicable)
  • Makeup essentials (if applicable)
  • Prescription medications (in original labeled containers)
  • Over-the-counter pain relief and antacids
  • Antihistamines / allergy medication
  • Melatonin or sleep aids (for jet lag)
  • First-aid kit (bandages, antiseptic wipes, antibiotic ointment)
  • Insect repellent
  • Hand sanitizer and disinfecting wipes

Comfort and Entertainment

  • Travel pillow and eye mask
  • Earplugs
  • Books, e-reader, or travel journal
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Snacks for travel days
  • Gum or mints

Safety and Organization

  • TSA-approved luggage locks
  • Packing cubes
  • Ziplock bags (for liquids and wet items)
  • Foldable extra bag (for souvenirs or laundry)
  • Waterproof phone case
  • Digital copies of all documents (email or cloud)
  • Luggage tags with contact info
