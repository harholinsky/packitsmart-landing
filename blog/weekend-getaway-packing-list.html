Weekend Getaway Packing List: Pack Light and Forget Nothing

Weekend trips are short, but forgetting essentials can ruin the experience. A simple weekend getaway packing list helps you pack light while staying prepared.

The Pack Light Rule

For weekend travel, focus only on essentials. Avoid packing items you will not realistically use.

Clothing Essentials

  • 2 casual outfits (mix-and-match tops and bottoms)
  • 1 dressier outfit for dinner or going out
  • 1 jacket, hoodie, or cardigan for layering
  • Underwear (1 per day + 1 spare)
  • Socks (1 pair per day)
  • Pajamas
  • 1 pair of comfortable walking shoes
  • 1 pair of dressier shoes or sandals (if needed)
  • Swimsuit (if applicable)

Pro tip: Wear your bulkiest outfit while traveling to save luggage space.

Toiletries and Personal Care

  • Toothbrush and toothpaste
  • Deodorant
  • Face wash and moisturizer
  • Sunscreen and lip balm
  • Shampoo and conditioner (only if accommodation doesn't provide)
  • Razor
  • Hair care essentials
  • Contact lenses and solution (if applicable)
  • Minimal makeup (if applicable)
  • Prescription medications
  • Basic pain relief (ibuprofen or similar)

Tech and Entertainment

  • Phone and charger
  • Portable power bank
  • Earbuds or headphones
  • Book or e-reader
  • Camera (optional)

Travel Essentials

  • ID / driver's license or passport (if flying)
  • Credit card and small amount of cash
  • Sunglasses
  • Small umbrella or rain jacket (check the forecast)
  • Tote bag or day bag for exploring
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Snacks for the road
  • Car charger (if driving)

Warm Weather Add-Ons

  • Extra sunscreen (water-resistant)
  • Bug spray
  • Sun hat
  • Sandals or water shoes

Cold Weather Add-Ons

  • Warm coat
  • Hat, scarf, and gloves
  • Wool socks
  • Waterproof boots
