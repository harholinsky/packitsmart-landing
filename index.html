About PackItSmart

PackItSmart generates packing lists based on real weather data for your destination. You tell it where and when — it figures out what you need.

Built for people who travel regularly and hate the "did I forget something?" feeling. The app handles the thinking so you can focus on the trip.

Available on iPhone and iPad. Android coming soon.
